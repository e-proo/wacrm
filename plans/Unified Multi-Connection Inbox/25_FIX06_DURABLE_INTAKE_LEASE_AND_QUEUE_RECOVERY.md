# FIX-06 — Durable Intake Lease & Queue Recovery

## Incident and scope
2026-11-27 hosted TEST: text completed in 17.11 s, image in 30.24 s; video persisted and visible with exact canonical source and seven returned stages (including message_received), but its receipt was held after 107.78 s. The existing finalizer rejected writes after the original 90 s lease. Four later encrypted receipts were still pending, never claimed. This is a queue head-of-line safety fence, not evidence of a PDF decoder failure.

## Design
- Migration 233: same immutable claim token, original intake_claimed_at, account, connection, pinned mode and payload. Heartbeat every 20 s, 90 s sliding lease, renewal capped at 5 minutes from original claim; exact live token and current route/credential checks. Cannot revive expired or terminal leases. Existing single-running-per-route constraint, FIFO, review block and no handler replay remain.
- Worker route maxDuration 300 s, local intake request timeout 310 s; server/platform may impose shorter limits. Renewal RPC timeout 10 s; no overlap, timer stopped in finally. Loss of lease never causes another business invocation. A non-cooperative handler may still finish after a platform timeout; the receipt and stage fences prevent replay, not an external exactly-once guarantee.
- Versioned claim entry point performs bounded (5 per tick) receipt-only recovery in canonical mode and delegates to the existing FIFO claim. Applying schema does not activate recovery on old workers. Requires exact signed receipt/observation, source/provider/native ID, verified single-connection epoch covering the complete effect (historical epochs allowed for receipt-only settlement), returned effect and final message_received stage with no incomplete stages, media URL for media, no canonical review or deferred handoff, no token/mode change. Unknown/nonmatching outcomes remain blocked. Completed proof derives processed_at from stored returned_at; stores recovered_at/from and retains digest and canonical/stage evidence, scrubs completed encrypted payload under existing policy. No synthetic actor/delivery proof, no send, no reprocessing. No changes to SELECT-only proof-table grants; receipt/source locks and exact historical proof recheck. Old code remains compatible with additive schema but must be stopped before rollout.
- Recovery is intentionally conservative: e.g. missing/deleted media, historical epoch closed before effect return, or missing final stage stay in review. New processing always requires current credential/session binding. A historical completed receipt is not a new route authorization. Do not disable canonical/durable gates while pending work is pinned to canonical. No Production modification.
